The Second Annual France Marseille / Toulouse Mission Reunion

Sur le Pont d'Avignon, l'on y danse, l'on y danse…

On the bridge of Avignon, everyone dances, all in a ring.

There is an old bridge in the south of France, the Pont d'Avignon, and an even older song about it. In the song, the bridge is where everyone gathers: l'on y danse tous en rond, all dancing in a ring.

That is the whole idea of our soirée. Our mission had its own bridges: between Marseille and Toulouse, between companions, between the Christmases we still carry. This December, come gather on ours.

Refreshments, carols by request, and a favorite two-minute Christmas memory to share. No RSVP required. Everyone is welcome, last-minute included.

Sunday, December 20 ✦ 6:00 to 8:00 PM ✦ Eagle Gate Apartments, adjacent to Temple Square
